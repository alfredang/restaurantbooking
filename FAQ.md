# Maison Lumière — Frequently Asked Questions

A reference document for the Maison Lumière restaurant website. Intended for retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) over customer-facing questions about dining, reservations, menu, accessibility, and policies.

---

## Reservations & Bookings

### How do I make a reservation?
Reservations can be made directly on our website through the **Reservations** section. Fill in your name, email, phone number, party size, preferred date, and time, and submit the form. You will see an on-screen confirmation immediately. For same-day bookings within two hours of your desired time, please call the restaurant directly at +33 1 42 60 00 00.

### How far in advance can I book?
We accept reservations up to **90 days in advance**. The date picker on our reservation form is constrained to today's date as the earliest selectable day — past dates cannot be selected.

### Can I book for a large group?
Our online form supports parties of up to **12 guests**. For groups of 13 or more, including private dining and full-venue buyouts, please email `events@maisonlumiere.example` with your date, group size, and any dietary requirements.

### What is your cancellation policy?
Cancellations made more than **24 hours before** your reservation are free of charge. Cancellations within 24 hours incur a €25 per-guest fee, which is charged to the card on file. No-shows are charged the full prix fixe menu price per guest.

### Do I need to confirm my reservation?
A confirmation email is sent automatically. We also send a reminder SMS 24 hours before your booking. If you do not receive a confirmation within 15 minutes of submitting the form, please check your spam folder or contact us.

### Can I modify my reservation after booking?
Yes. Reply to your confirmation email with the requested change (new time, party size, etc.) at least 24 hours in advance. Same-day modifications are best handled by phone.

## Hours & Location

### What are your hours of operation?
- **Tuesday – Thursday**: 18:00 – 22:30 (dinner only)
- **Friday – Saturday**: 12:00 – 14:30 (lunch), 18:00 – 23:00 (dinner)
- **Sunday**: 12:00 – 15:00 (brunch service)
- **Monday**: Closed

The kitchen takes last orders 45 minutes before closing.

### Where are you located?
Maison Lumière is located at **14 Rue des Lumières, 75004 Paris, France**, in the Marais district. The nearest Métro stations are Saint-Paul (Line 1) and Hôtel de Ville (Lines 1 and 11), both within a five-minute walk.

### Is there parking nearby?
There is no dedicated parking. The closest public parking garages are Parking Baudoyer and Parking Lobau, both within 300 meters. We recommend public transport or taxi/rideshare for evening service.

## Menu & Cuisine

### What style of cuisine do you serve?
Maison Lumière serves **modern French cuisine** rooted in classical technique. Our menu changes seasonally, with an emphasis on producers from Île-de-France, Normandy, and the Loire Valley.

### Do you offer a tasting menu?
Yes. Our **seven-course tasting menu** is available every evening at €165 per person, with an optional wine pairing at €95. The tasting menu must be ordered by the entire table and should be requested at least 48 hours in advance.

### Can you accommodate dietary restrictions?
Absolutely. We can accommodate **vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, nut-free, and shellfish-free** diets with notice. Please note dietary requirements in the "Special Requests" field of your reservation, or email us at least 48 hours before your visit. Severe allergies should be communicated by phone.

### Do you have a children's menu?
We welcome children and offer a three-course children's menu at €35 for guests aged 12 and under. High chairs are available on request.

### Are the dish photos on the site accurate?
Yes — every dish image on our website depicts the actual plating of that course as served in the restaurant during the current season. Photos are updated when the menu changes.

### What signature dishes are you known for?
Our most-requested dishes include the **Canard à l'Orange Confit**, the **Bouillabaisse Marseillaise**, and the **Soufflé au Grand Marnier**. Availability rotates with the season.

## Wine & Beverages

### Do you have a sommelier?
Yes. Our head sommelier, Élise Marchand, oversees a list of approximately 600 references with a focus on Burgundy, the Rhône, and natural wines from the Jura. Pairing recommendations are available at every price point.

### Can I bring my own wine (corkage)?
We permit corkage for bottles not on our list, at €45 per 750ml bottle, with a maximum of two bottles per table. Please notify us in advance.

### Do you have non-alcoholic pairings?
Yes. Our non-alcoholic pairing flight (€55) features house-made shrubs, kombuchas, and infusions designed to complement the tasting menu.

## Dress Code & Atmosphere

### Is there a dress code?
Smart casual to formal. We ask that guests refrain from athletic wear, beachwear, and visible activewear. Jackets are appreciated but not required for gentlemen.

### Is the restaurant suitable for a romantic occasion?
Yes — Maison Lumière is frequently chosen for anniversaries, proposals, and milestone celebrations. Mention the occasion in your reservation notes and we will do our best to make it memorable (a complimentary dessert plate is offered for confirmed celebrations).

### How loud is the dining room?
The dining room is designed for conversation, with acoustic panels and an average sound level around 65 dB during peak service. We do not play amplified music.

## Accessibility

### Is the restaurant wheelchair accessible?
Yes. The main entrance is step-free, and the dining room and accessible restroom are on the ground floor. Please indicate accessibility needs in your reservation so we can assign a suitable table.

### Do you accommodate guests with visual or hearing impairments?
Large-print menus are available on request, and our staff are trained to describe dishes in detail. We can also provide a written description of the tasting menu in advance by email.

### Are service animals welcome?
Yes — service animals are always welcome. Please let us know in advance so we can prepare the table appropriately.

## Payment & Pricing

### What payment methods do you accept?
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and cash (EUR). We do not accept personal cheques or cryptocurrency.

### Is service included?
Service is included in all prices, in line with French custom. Additional gratuity is appreciated but never expected.

### Do you offer gift cards?
Yes. Gift cards in denominations of €100, €200, and €500 are available for purchase at the host stand or by emailing `gifts@maisonlumiere.example`. They are valid for 18 months from the date of issue.

## Private Events & Catering

### Can I host a private event?
Our private dining room **Le Salon Doré** seats up to 24 guests and is available for lunch and dinner bookings. Full-venue buyouts (up to 70 guests) are available on Mondays and select Sunday evenings.

### Do you offer off-site catering?
We offer off-site catering for events of 20 or more guests within Paris and the inner suburbs. Please email `events@maisonlumiere.example` for a custom proposal.

## Website & Technical

### The reservation form isn't submitting — what should I do?
The form validates each field on submit and displays an error message beneath any invalid field. Common issues:
- **Date in the past**: the date picker only allows today or later.
- **Party size out of range**: must be between 1 and 12.
- **Invalid email format**: must include `@` and a domain.
- **Phone number too short**: must be at least 7 digits.

If the form continues to fail after correcting all highlighted errors, please call us directly.

### Does the site work on mobile?
Yes. The site is fully responsive, with a dedicated mobile navigation menu accessible via the hamburger icon in the top right.

### Does the site support reduced motion?
Yes. If your operating system has "reduce motion" enabled, all fade-in animations and smooth scrolling are disabled automatically.

### Is my data stored when I submit the form?
The current reservation form is **client-side only** — your information is used to display an on-screen confirmation but is not transmitted to a server. (Note: this applies to the website demo; production deployments integrate with our reservation backend.)
